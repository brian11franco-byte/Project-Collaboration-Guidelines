Methodology

The Concept of Horizontal Alignment

Horizontal alignment is the deliberate coordination of curriculum standards, themes, and assessments across different subjects during the same teaching cycle. Rather than learning subjects in isolation, students experience a unified, connected pathway that highlights real-world relationships.

By synchronizing lesson pacing, teachers ensure that prerequisite skills in one subject (e.g. money calculation in Math) are taught exactly when they are needed for application in another (e.g. purchasing raw prototyping goods in Science).

Pedagogical Benefits

💡 High Cognitive Transfer

Students see immediate connections between theories (e.g., studying electrical circuit components in Science and building architectural models in Design).

📉 Student Workload Reduction

Instead of submitting separate projects for different classes, students design a single interdisciplinary project evaluated across multiple distinct subject rubrics.

🤝 Subject Connections

In traditional schedules, students study topics in isolated bubbles, making it difficult to understand practical applications. Horizontal alignment solves this by establishing cross-cutting themes (like "The Shopping Center" or "Light and Shadows"). The classroom becomes a dynamic learning lab where theoretical science questions are resolved using math coordinates and ELA descriptive vocabulary in real-time.

📉 Student Workload Decrease

One of the most immediate benefits of horizontal alignment is the reduction of assessment stress. By aligning curriculum maps, teachers replace separate end-of-term exams with a single, high-fidelity interdisciplinary project. Graded independently on subject-specific criteria, this combined requirement eliminates redundant work and allows students to focus their efforts on producing high-quality work.

Collaboration Readiness Checklist

Verify if your collaborative unit is ready for classroom execution:

  • Both calendars have been cross-referenced for common terms.
  • The connecting theme accommodates objectives from both curricula without sacrificing standard rigor.
  • Math/analytical prerequisites are scheduled at least 1 week before application.
  • A shared rubric has been defined with separate grading keys for each subject.
  • We scheduled a weekly 15-minute sync to check student pace.

2 Subjects Primary X (1st Sem)

Body Puppet Intro Project

Students learn body part names and locations in Science, construct a paper body puppet to build fine motor skills, and introduce themselves using new vocabulary in English and Bahasa Indonesia.

Horizontal Alignment Points:
  • Science guides the physical puppet creation and biological naming of parts.
  • Bahasa Indonesia and English coordinate self-introduction parameters (name, age, hobi).
  • Final Practical Assessment: Introducing self to the class using the constructed paper puppet as a visual aid.
3 Subjects Primary X (1st Sem Final)

Miniature Stage Drama

Students build a miniature physical theater stage using recycled materials in Science, and write and perform a script based on a historical/religious narrative in English, Bahasa, and Religious Studies.

Horizontal Alignment Points:
  • Science teaches properties of recycled materials to build a durable stage and props.
  • English and Bahasa coordinate procedure writing and narrative monologue movements.
  • RS provides script materials based on historical stories (e.g. Joshua and Caleb).
4 Subjects Primary X (2nd Sem Final)

Water Games Day & Buoyancy

A full-day interactive sports and science event. Students perform time-reading on clock faces, test floatation devices in Science, play obstacle games in PE, and tap rhythm beats in Music.

Horizontal Alignment Points:
  • Math teaches analog reading (o'clock clock product) used to verify timers in PE.
  • Science covers floating, sinking, and building a simple floating device.
  • Music and PE coordinate body percussion beats and physical gross motor movements.
4 Subjects Primary 3 (1st Sem Final)

My Book of Light

A collaborative storybook final project. Students study light and shadow properties in Science, draft creative narratives in ELA, build coordinates word problems in Math, and sculpt 3D shadow models in Art.

Horizontal Alignment Points:
  • Science conducts light source experimentations; ELA applies descriptive adjectives to narrative settings.
  • Math designs algebraic word problems built on story elements.
  • Arts sculpts clay landmarks that act as shadows on the book pages.
5 Subjects Primary 2 (2nd Sem Final)

Shopping Center

A miniature town center construction project. Students design a new electrical appliance blueprint in Science, handle money transactions in Math, map city spaces in English, and execute buying dialogues in Chinese.

Horizontal Alignment Points:
  • Math teaches money and calculations exactly when students buy prototype supplies.
  • Science builds simple electrical circuit loops inside clay/carton structures.
  • Languages structure buying dialogues (CL) and job/location cards (BI/English).
